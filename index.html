FAQ

Before you join.

What version does ShiftSMP use?

The server runs Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.11 and players can join starting from version 1.21.6.

What is the server IP?

shiftsmp.shockbyte.xyz

How many homes do players get?

Currently, every player gets one home. This applies to the current rank setup as well.

Is ShiftSMP pay-to-win?

The ranks are designed primarily around customization, cosmetics and convenience rather than major combat advantages.

What does the scale perk do?

The configured player scale limits are 2× for Elite, 3× for Master and 5× for Supreme through the server's Attribute system.

Where can I get announcements?

Join the ShiftSMP Discord community for announcements, updates and community information.
