CREATORS

Media on ShiftSMP.

Content creators can have their own place in the ShiftSMP community.

Media Rank

Create. Play. Share.

The Media rank is designed for approved content creators who make ShiftSMP content. It provides dedicated styling and cosmetic perks without granting staff powers.

Important

Media ≠ Staff

Media is a creator/community rank, not a moderation rank. Media members do not receive Moderator or Manager permissions simply because they have the Media role.

FAQ

Questions?

A few quick answers before you join.

What Minecraft version can I use?

ShiftSMP accepts Minecraft Java Edition versions starting from 1.21.6. The server itself runs version 1.21.11.

What is the server IP?

The server address is shiftsmp.shockbyte.xyz.

Does everyone get a home?

Yes. The standard setup gives players one home location. Premium ranks do not receive unlimited homes.

Is ShiftSMP pay-to-win?

The goal is to keep rank perks focused on customization, convenience and cosmetics rather than giving players huge gameplay advantages.

Is Media a staff rank?

No. Media is a creator/community rank. Moderation and management are handled by the actual staff ranks.

Where can I join the community?

Join the ShiftSMP Discord to meet other players, get announcements and stay updated.
