Frequently Asked Questions

Need to know something before joining?

What version is ShiftSMP?

ShiftSMP is built for Minecraft Java Edition. Check the Discord for the current supported server version.

Do I need a rank to play?

No. Everyone can play the core ShiftSMP survival experience without purchasing a rank.

Are the ranks monthly?

No. Elite, Master and Supreme are one-time purchases rather than monthly subscriptions.

Are the ranks pay-to-win?

The ranks are designed primarily around quality-of-life and cosmetic perks rather than giving players overwhelming gameplay advantages.

Where can I get a rank?

Rank purchases and information are handled through the official ShiftSMP Discord.

How do I join the community?

Join the ShiftSMP Discord using the button below. You'll find server information, announcements and community updates there.
